# Candidate Examination Privacy Policy Template

*Instructions for Institutions: This template provides standard disclosure text aligned with GDPR, FERPA, and international data protection laws (`O-04`). Customise the bracketed text `[like this]` before publishing to your candidates.*

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When you take an examination on Pariksha administered by **[Institution / Organization Name]**, the following categories of data may be processed:

1. **Identification Details:** Your name, student or employee ID, email address, and any specific registration fields required for your examination.
2. **Responses and Submissions:** The answers, numerical calculations, essays, uploaded files, and code submitted during your exam sitting.
3. **Examination Telemetry & Integrity Logs:** Timestamps of question visits, answer save events, window blur or tab-switch events, and fullscreen exits.
4. **Visual Proctoring Data (If Activated by Your Exam):**
   - An initial reference photo taken prior to exam commencement.
   - Periodic low-resolution webcam snapshots sampled during testing to verify candidate presence.
   - *Note:* Pariksha does not perform automated algorithmic facial biometric identification or automated disqualification. All integrity logs and snapshots are reviewed exclusively by human proctors and instructors.

---

## 2. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

Your personal data is processed solely for the purposes of:
- Verifying candidate identity and eligibility.
- Delivering fair, secure, and authenticated academic assessments.
- Detecting and preventing academic dishonesty during examinations.
- Grading submissions and computing official academic results.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

- **Encryption:** All responses and visual snapshots are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest in dedicated storage buckets.
- **Tenant Isolation:** Your data is strictly isolated within **[Institution Name]**'s workspace and is never accessible to other organisations.
- **Automated Deletion:** Visual proctoring snapshots and telemetry logs are scheduled for automated cryptographic deletion **[e.g. 30 days]** following examination completion, in accordance with our institutional retention schedule.

---

## 4. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Request access to the responses and proctoring logs recorded during your exam.
- Request rectification of inaccurate personal records.
- Lodge a formal inquiry with **[Institution Data Protection Officer / Contact Email]**.

For technical or examination inquiries, contact **[support@institution.example.org]**.
