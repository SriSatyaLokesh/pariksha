What is Pariksha?

Pariksha is an open-source exam platform you deploy on your own Cloudflare or Docker account, so candidate data never reaches a vendor. It is pre-alpha as of October 2026 — the formats, the schemas, a worked example and the scoring engine are published; the application lands with v0.1.
